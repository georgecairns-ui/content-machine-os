# Skills

Saved know-how for running your content plan. You don't need to remember any of this: ask in your own words and Claude picks the right one.

Each skill is a folder with a `SKILL.md` inside. Claude reads them from here whenever this folder is open.

## Your content plan

In Claude Code, every skill is also a command: type `/` and its name, for example `/plan-my-content`.

| Skill | Say something like | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `research-my-market` | "Research my market", "what are people searching for?", "look at my competitors", or press **Refresh research** in the app | Claude reads public web pages only: what your customers search for, what's in season, and what your competitors do well. It saves what it finds, with where it found it, then updates your own what-works skill. It never logs in anywhere |
| `build-my-content-skill` | "Update what works for us" | Claude saves what it knows about your audience and your industry as a skill of your own, and packs it up in `skills-to-upload` so you can add it to the Claude app |
| `plan-my-content` | "Plan my content", "plan next week", "fill my calendar", or press **Plan next weeks** in the app | Claude fills the next weeks with posts, a day and a score for each platform, and tells you what it needs from you. It writes every post straight away, and holds back only the ones that need a case study or an answer from you |
| `write-posts` | "Write my waiting posts", "write this up", or **Ask Claude to write it** in the app | Claude writes each platform's version in your voice, scores it, and adds a photo, video or case study to your To do list if it needs one |
| `turn-this-into-posts` | "I've got an idea", "turn this into a post", "make this into content" | Claude saves what you said as an idea, or a planned post if you say when |
| `use-my-case-study` | "Here's a case study", "write up this job" | Claude turns a real job into posts and asks you for a photo if there isn't one. It never makes up a result |
| `content-check-in` | "What's due today?", "what do I need to post?", "what's outstanding?" | Claude tells you what to post, what to approve and what it still needs from you |
| `ive-posted-it` | "I've posted it", "that's gone out" | Claude marks it as posted on your calendar and in your Health check |
| `sort-content-brain-dump` | Press **Brain dump** on any page (or V) and talk | Claude turns the content part of a brain dump into ideas, planned posts and updates |

Your own what-works skill appears here too once the research has run, named after your business, such as `fern-and-finch-what-works`. It stays on your computer.

## Keeping things organised

| Skill | Say something like | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `sort-brain-dump` | Press **Brain dump** on any page (or V) and talk | Claude sorts it and sends each part to the right tool |
| `file-it` | "Save this file" | Claude suggests a folder and a name, and files it once you agree |
| `connect-a-tool` | "Connect my team's shared folder" | Claude walks you through connecting an app safely |
| `match-my-brand` | "Make it match my brand" | Your colours, font and logo on the app, from your website or brand guidelines |
| `setup` | `/setup` | Runs the setup, or carries on where it left off |
| `open-the-app` | "Open my content machine", "open the app" | Starts the app if needed and gives you the link, http://localhost:4747 |
| `get-updates` | "Get the latest version", "any updates?" | Your copy brought up to date with a safe backup first. Your records, notes, skills and brand stay exactly as they are |
| `keep-context-fresh` | (automatic) | When you mention something new about the business, Claude offers to update its notes |

## Writing

| Skill | Say something like | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `human-email` | "Make this sound less robotic" | Any draft is checked so it reads like a person wrote it |
| `write-like-me` | "Learn how I write" | Claude learns your style (with permission) and writes in it |
| `polite-chaser` | "Chase this up for me" | A chaser at the right level of firmness, with the specifics |

## The rules every skill follows

- Claude never posts for you. It writes, plans and scores; you check each post and post it yourself.
- Research reads public web pages only. Claude never logs in, follows or messages anyone for you.
- Claude never makes up a result, a number or a customer's words. If it needs one, it asks you.
- Everything stays in this folder or in the apps you've connected.

## Adding your own

Ask Claude: "Make a skill for how we do X." It'll write one in this folder using the same format, and show it to you first.
