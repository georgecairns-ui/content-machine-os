# Marketing notes: Content Machine OS

## In one sentence

A content plan that fills your calendar from what your customers search for, writes each post in your voice and scores it for each platform, and leaves you to approve it and post it yourself.

## Before and after, for the owner

**Before:** you know you should be posting. You open Instagram or LinkedIn, stare at a blank box, post something twice in a fortnight, then nothing for 3 months. You don't know what your customers are looking for, you glance at a competitor's feed now and then, and every post takes longer to write than it should.

**After:** you open one calendar and your next 2 weeks are planned. Each post is written in your voice for each platform, with a score out of 10 for where it should do best, and a plain note on what Claude needs from you: a photo of the finished job, a 30 second video, a customer's story. Each morning your To do list says what to post. You press Copy, press Open, paste, post, and press "I've posted it". Claude never posts for you.

Time saved: to check. Do not quote a figure until it has been measured with real users.

## 3 things to show in a 60-second video

1. **The calendar filling.** Press "Plan next 2 weeks" and the posts appear on the week, with their platforms and scores. Drag one to another day.
2. **One post, 3 platforms.** Open a post and flick through its versions: a short LinkedIn post, an Instagram caption and a Google Business Profile update, each with its character count and its score and the reason for it.
3. **The To do list.** "Post to Instagram today" with the Copy and Open buttons, and "Add a photo of the finished patio" waiting for you. Press "I've posted it" and the Health check moves.
