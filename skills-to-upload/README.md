# Skills to upload

During setup, Claude researches your market and writes you a skill of your own: saved instructions about your audience and your industry, such as what your customers search for, what works for businesses like yours on each platform and the hooks that do well. Your own name goes on it, for example `your-business-what-works`.

It already works in Claude Code in this folder. The file here is the same skill, packed up so you can add it to Claude itself and use it in every conversation, including the Claude app on your phone.

## How to add it to Claude

1. Open Claude (claude.ai or the Claude app).
2. Go to **Customize**, then **Skills**.
3. Press **+**, then **Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**, and choose the `.zip` file in this folder.

Skills need a paid Claude plan with code execution switched on (in **Settings**, under **Capabilities**).

If the menus look different, ask Claude: "Where do I upload a skill?"
